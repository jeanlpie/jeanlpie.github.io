My research combines in-vivo electrophysiology, optogenetics, complex behavior in rodents, advanced statistical modelling and ML-based neuronal decoding.
I am interested in multisensory perception, systems neuroscience and applications for Brain Computer Interfaces in (pre)clinical settings.
